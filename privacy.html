Privacy Policy

Shield Our Elders helps people check suspicious messages, calls, links, QR codes, screenshots, voicemail transcripts, and payment requests. This policy explains what the app handles and how it is used.

What you choose to provide

The app reviews only content you choose to enter, upload, scan, or share with it. That may include pasted message text, email text, URLs, phone numbers, QR code contents, screenshots, audio files, voicemail transcripts, trusted contact details, settings, learning progress, and support messages.

What the app does not automatically read

Shield Our Elders does not automatically read calls, text messages, emails, contacts, photos, microphone audio, location, browsing history, bank accounts, passwords, or payment apps. You stay in control of what gets checked.

Online safety checks

Some checks may send selected content to service providers that help run scam review, screenshot text extraction, speech-to-text, hosting, security, analytics, or reliability services. These checks are used for app functionality, not advertising or tracking.

Trusted contacts

Trusted contact names and phone numbers are stored so users can call or text a family member, caregiver, neighbor, or other trusted person. The app does not contact them unless the user chooses that action.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers needed to operate selected features, when required by law, for safety and security, or when a user chooses to share information with a trusted contact through their own phone or messaging app.

Contact

For privacy questions, corrections, or deletion requests, email projectshieldourelders@gmail.com.
