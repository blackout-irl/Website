The idea

Scams work because they rush people.

Shield Our Elders teaches one habit: pause, verify, then act. Seniors practice real calls, texts, emails, links, QR codes, payment requests, and family emergency scams.

Not fear. Clear next steps.

Baseline surveys and scam-recognition checks show what seniors are really seeing locally.

02

Practice together

Short scenarios teach pressure signs: urgency, secrecy, fake authority, codes, links, and payments.
