A student project for families

Wait. Let's check it first.

Scams work because they rush people. Shield Our Elders gives someone a quiet minute to check a message, a phone call, or a payment request.

See an example Contact the team
Fridge note version Do not send money while someone is rushing you.

Built for senior centers, libraries, and families who want a simple answer to one question: “Should I trust this?”

What it checks

A warning should be easy to read.

We kept the core questions short because stress makes long screens harder to use.

Texts, emails, screenshots

Paste it in. The app points to the part that feels wrong.

Phone calls

Did they ask for money, secrecy, a code, remote access, or threaten you?

Payments

Gift cards, crypto, wires, Zelle, Cash App, and Venmo are treated carefully.

Links and QR codes

Check where it goes before opening it, especially for banks, tolls, and deliveries.

Sample check

The answer should sound like a person.

No dramatic score. No giant red screen. Just why it looks suspicious and what to do next.

Practice message

Text message

You won a free iPhone. Send your credit card details to claim.

Likely scam It asks for private payment info.

Real prizes do not need your credit card number in a text.

Trusted contact

The app should not replace family.

It should make it easier to reach family. Users can save a daughter, son, caregiver, neighbor, or bank number and send a simple text:

“I received a suspicious call. Can you help me verify it?”

Bring it to your group
