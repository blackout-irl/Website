Main check

A warning that explains itself.

No mystery score. No scary wall of text. The app points out the exact signals and gives a next step.

Live example
Text message

You won a free iPhone. Send your credit card details to claim.

Likely scam It asks for private payment info.

Real prizes do not need your credit card number in a text.

Trusted contact

One tap can bring in a real person.

The safest answer is often simple: call a daughter, son, caregiver, neighbor, or bank using a number the user already trusts.

Text ready

I received a suspicious call. Can you help me verify it?
