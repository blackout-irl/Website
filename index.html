Five questions

The rush is the signal.

Money, secrecy, codes, remote access, threats. If one shows up, the safest next move is to pause.

Before money moves

No gift cards. No panic transfers.

Gift cards, crypto, wires, Zelle, Cash App, and Venmo are hard to reverse. The app slows the decision down.
