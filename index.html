Field guide no. 01 / scam safety

One pause before the money leaves.

Shield Our Elders is a student-built app for older adults and the people who look out for them. It checks suspicious texts, calls, links, QR codes, and payment requests in plain language.

most scams start ordinary
rule of thumb If they rush you, stop.
Stop Check Call someone trusted

Why this exists

Scammers do not need better tech. They need panic.

What they say

“Do not tell anyone. Read me the code.”

What the app asks

Did they ask for secrecy? Did they ask for a verification code?

What the user sees

Hang up. Call your trusted contact. Do not send money yet.

Sample check

It should explain the red flag, not just label it.

Seniors should not have to decode a score. The app gives the reason, then the next step.

Text message

You won a free iPhone. Send your credit card details to claim.

Likely scam It asks for private payment info.

Real prizes do not need your credit card number in a text.

What is inside

A few useful checks, kept short.

Message check

Paste a text or upload a screenshot.

Call questions

Money, secrecy, codes, threats, remote access.

Link and QR check

Look before opening.

Payment pause

Gift cards, crypto, wires, Zelle, Cash App, Venmo.

Trusted contact

The best safety feature is still another person.

Save a daughter, son, caregiver, neighbor, or bank number. When a call feels wrong, the app can help send:

“I received a suspicious call. Can you help me verify it?”

Practice

A few examples before the real thing.

Practice question

Your bank account is locked. Tap this link now to confirm your login.

Suspicious link. Call the bank from the card.
