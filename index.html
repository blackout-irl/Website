The habit

Stop. Check. Ask. Recover.

  1. 01
    End the pressure.

    Hang up. Do not click. Do not send money.

  2. 02
    Check the warning signs.

    Codes, secrecy, fake urgency, unusual payment requests.

  3. 03
    Ask a trusted person.

    Call family, a caregiver, or the official company number.

  4. 04
    Recover without getting tricked again.

    Bank, passwords, reports, and recovery scam warnings.
