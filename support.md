# Shield Our Elders app support

For help, email [projectshieldourelders@gmail.com](mailto:projectshieldourelders@gmail.com). Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, the feature you opened, and what happened. Leave out passwords, full account numbers, verification codes, and other private details.

## Check a message

Open Message on Home. Paste or type the words you received, then start the check. The iPhone app checks the text on your device. It does not require an account or an analysis server.

## Check a screenshot

Open Photo on Home and choose a screenshot with readable message text. The app reads the words before checking them. If the words are wrong, use Edit text to correct them. A blank or unreadable image cannot produce a meaningful safety result; type the message instead.

## Check a saved recording

Open Call on Home, tap Start a check, then Recording. Choose Video from Photos, Record a video, Voice Memo, or Audio file. For Voice Memos, select the memo in Apple's Voice Memos app, tap More (…), Share, then Shield or Open in Shield. If Shield is not listed, save the memo to Files and use Audio file.

Record a video asks for camera and microphone permission only when chosen. The app extracts video sound before transcription; it does not analyze video images or record live phone calls. Choose clear speech up to 3 minutes long. A video without sound, damaged recording, or longer clip cannot be checked; trim it or choose another one.

Transcription requires supported offline English speech recognition. You can review and edit the transcript before another check. If recognition is unavailable or unclear, type or paste what was said instead.

## Read a result aloud

Tap the speaker on a check result. Settings → Read aloud includes a Try the voice button, reading speed, and optional automatic result reading. Turn up your media volume. The iPhone playback session allows requested speech even when Silent mode is on. Leaving the screen or putting the app in the background stops reading.

## Safety reminders and alerts

Open Settings → Reminders & alerts. Enable notifications, then choose Weekly or Every 2 weeks. The first reminder is in 7 or 14 days. The app confirms successful scheduling and explains permission errors. Send a test notification to check delivery after about 5 seconds. Focus and Scheduled Summary can delay presentation; Open iPhone notification settings lets you review the OS controls. Alerts are triggered by checks you run, not by monitoring incoming calls or messages.

## A result looks wrong

The app can miss scams and can flag ordinary messages. A low warning level does not prove a request is safe. Before sending money, sharing account access or giving someone a code, verify using a number or official app you already know. Ask someone you trust for a second opinion.

## Remove saved information

Open Settings and choose Delete all my data to remove locally saved app information. You can manage photo, speech-recognition and notification permissions in iOS Settings.

## Money or account details were already shared

Contact your bank or card issuer using the number on your card or statement. The app is educational and is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
